How it works

From first meeting to final accounts in three steps

ParaPlan replaces the notebook, the calculator and the long WhatsApp threads.

  1. 1

    Create your event

    Name your festival, set a chanda goal and add your committee members.

  2. 2

    Collect and plan

    Record chanda, assign volunteers and log spending as it happens.

  3. 3

    Share and close the books

    Send live updates to residents and download a final report when the festival ends.
